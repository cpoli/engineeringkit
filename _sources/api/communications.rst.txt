engineeringkit.communications
=============================


.. include:: /_generated/nav/communications.rst

Decibel conversions; wavelength, Friis free-space propagation and decibel
link budgets; Johnson-Nyquist thermal noise and the cascaded noise factor;
Nyquist signalling, Hartley's law, binary entropy and Shannon capacity; AM,
DSB-SC and FM modulation with Carson's bandwidth rule; BPSK/QPSK and their
AWGN bit-error rates (``scipy.special.erfc``); the Hamming(7,4) code; and
OFDM via ``numpy.fft``.
