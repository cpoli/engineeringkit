engineeringkit.electrical_engineering
=====================================

.. include:: /_generated/nav/electrical_engineering.rst

Ohm's law, series and parallel equivalents, the Wheatstone bridge and
modified nodal analysis; Thévenin and Norton equivalents and maximum power
transfer; RC and RL transients; phasors, complex impedance, series
resonance and the ideal transformer; lossless and lossy transmission lines,
the distortionless condition, reflection coefficients and input impedance;
real, reactive and three-phase power, symmetrical components and
Gauss-Seidel load flow (linear systems by ``numpy.linalg``, the RLC step
response by ``scipy.signal``).
