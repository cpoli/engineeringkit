His student Heinrich Blasius (1908) reduced the flat-plate layer to the ordinary differential equation f&#x27;&#x27;&#x27; + \tfrac12 f f&#x27;&#x27; = 0. The result, C_f = 0.074\,Re_L^{-1/5} (~engineeringkit.aerodynamics.skin_friction_turbulent), decays far more slowly with Reynolds number than the laminar 1.328\,Re_L^{-1/2}; a turbulent layer can carry several times the friction drag. This example compares the two and the payoff of delaying transition.">
