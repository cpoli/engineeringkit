    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="Martin Kutta (1902) and Nikolai Joukowski (1906) showed that the lift per unit span on any two-dimensional body in a steady inviscid stream is L&#x27; = \rho V \Gamma, where \Gamma is the circulation around the body (~engineeringkit.aerodynamics.kutta_joukowski_lift). The cleanest check is potential flow past a cylinder with circulation (a model of the Magnus effect): integrating the Bernoulli surface pressure around the cylinder reproduces \rho V\Gamma exactly, and the drag integrates to zero (d&#x27;Alembert&#x27;s paradox).">
