    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="Louis Breguet&#x27;s range formula (1923) follows from three facts: thrust equals drag, drag equals weight divided by L/D, and fuel flow is the thrust-specific fuel consumption times thrust. Integrating the weight loss gives R = \frac{V}{g c_T}\frac{L}{D}\ln\frac{W_i}{W_f} (~engineeringkit.aerodynamics.breguet_range): range is the product of engine efficiency, aerodynamic efficiency and a logarithmic fuel fraction. This example checks it against a time-stepped cruise and shows why airliners fly near the best-glide lift coefficient.">
