    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="Early telephone engineers measured loss in &quot;miles of standard cable&quot;, a unit that depended on frequency. In 1924 the Bell System replaced it with the transmission unit, ten times the base-ten logarithm of a power ratio, and in 1929 W. H. Martin named it the decibel. Logarithms turn the products of gains and losses along a line into sums, so a long chain of amplifiers and cables can be budgeted by addition. db, dbm and their inverses convert both ways.">
