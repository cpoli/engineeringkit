For two antipodal signals the answer is a correlator (or matched filter) followed by a sign decision, with bit-error rate Q(\sqrt{2E_b/N_0}) (~engineeringkit.communications.ber_bpsk_awgn, q_function). Cosine and sine carriers at the same frequency are orthogonal, so each can carry an independent BPSK stream. Gray-labelled QPSK (~engineeringkit.communications.qpsk) sends two bits per symbol in the bandwidth of one. Its bit-error rate per unit E_b/N_0 equals that of BPSK (~engineeringkit.communications.ber_qpsk_awgn).">
