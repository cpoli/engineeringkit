    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="How stiff is a mixture of two phases? Woldemar Voigt (1889) averaged the elastic constants of the crystals in a polycrystal on the assumption that all of them strain alike; Adolf Reuss (1929) averaged the compliances on the assumption that all carry the same stress. For a fibre composite the two assumptions are exact along and across the fibres (~engineeringkit.materials.rule_of_mixtures_E_longitudinal, rule_of_mixtures_E_transverse), and Hill (1952) proved that every other arrangement lies between them. The example builds both from springs and checks a glass-epoxy laminate.">
