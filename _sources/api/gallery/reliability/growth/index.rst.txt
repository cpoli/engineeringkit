    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="James Duane, at General Electric&#x27;s Motor and Generator Department, plotted the failure records of five development programmes, from hydraulic devices to aircraft generators. On log-log paper the cumulative MTBF, total test time over total failures, rose along a straight line, each programme with a slope near 0.5. A straight line is a power law, \theta_c = K t^\alpha (~engineeringkit.reliability.reliability_growth_duane), so a few months of data predict how much more testing a reliability target needs. Because the failure count is N(t) = t^{1-\alpha}/K, the current failure rate is dN/dt, and the instantaneous MTBF is the cumulative one divided by 1 - \alpha.">
