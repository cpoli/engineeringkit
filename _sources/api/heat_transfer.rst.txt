engineeringkit.heat_transfer
============================


.. include:: /_generated/nav/heat_transfer.rst

Fourier's law and thermal resistances (plane, cylindrical and spherical
walls, composite walls, critical insulation radius); Harper-Brown fins;
finite-difference conduction by the explicit, implicit and Crank-Nicolson
schemes (``scipy.linalg.solve_banded``); Newton cooling, lumped and
semi-infinite transients (``scipy.special.erf``) and Heisler's plane-wall
series; the Dittus-Boelter and Churchill-Chu correlations; Planck,
Stefan-Boltzmann and Wien blackbody laws, gray-surface exchange and view
factors; Neumann's solution of the Stefan problem, Nusselt film
condensation and Zuber's critical heat flux; and LMTD and
effectiveness-NTU heat exchangers.
