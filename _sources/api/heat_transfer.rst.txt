engineeringkit.heat_transfer
============================


.. include:: /_generated/nav/heat_transfer.rst

Fourier's law and thermal resistances (plane, cylindrical and spherical
walls, composite walls, critical insulation radius); Harper-Brown fins with
adiabatic and convecting tips; finite-difference conduction by the explicit,
implicit and Crank-Nicolson schemes (``scipy.linalg.solve_banded``) and
steady 2-D conduction (``scipy.sparse``); conduction shape factors; Newton
cooling, lumped and semi-infinite transients (``scipy.special.erf``) and
Heisler's plane-wall, cylinder and sphere series; flat plates (Pohlhausen),
cylinders (Churchill-Bernstein) and tube banks (Žukauskas) in forced flow,
laminar tube flow and the entry length (Graetz, Hausen), the Dittus-Boelter
and Churchill-Chu correlations and the Chilton-Colburn analogy; Planck,
Stefan-Boltzmann and Wien blackbody laws, gray-surface exchange, view
factors and Oppenheim's radiation network with shields; Neumann's solution
of the Stefan problem, Nusselt film condensation, Rohsenow's nucleate
boiling and Zuber's critical heat flux; and LMTD and effectiveness-NTU heat
exchangers in parallel, counter, cross and shell-and-tube flow with the
LMTD correction factor.
