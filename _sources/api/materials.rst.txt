engineeringkit.materials
========================


.. include:: /_generated/nav/materials.rst

Crystal structures, packing factor, theoretical density and Bragg
diffraction; Fick's laws, the Arrhenius diffusivity and carburizing with
the error function (``scipy.special.erf``); the lever rule and Avrami
kinetics; Brinell and Vickers hardness; normal stress and strain, Hooke's law, Lamé's isotropic constants and
Duhamel's thermal stress; resilience, toughness and the measured
stress-strain curve with its offset yield strength; principal stresses and
the Tresca and von Mises yield criteria in three dimensions; the Voigt,
Reuss and Halpin-Tsai composite moduli and classical laminate theory;
Basquin's S-N law, the Gerber, Goodman and Soderberg mean-stress lines,
Marin's endurance-limit factors, the Coffin-Manson strain-life curve and
Palmgren-Miner damage; stress concentration, notch sensitivity and Neuber's
rule; Weibull's strength of brittle materials; Griffith's fracture stress, Irwin's stress intensity
factor and Paris crack growth integrated over a crack's life
(``scipy.integrate.cumulative_trapezoid``); Norton creep and the
Larson-Miller parameter; Frenkel's ideal shear strength, the Peierls stress,
Hall-Petch strengthening and the Ramberg-Osgood curve.
