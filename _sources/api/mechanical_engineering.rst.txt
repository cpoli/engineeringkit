engineeringkit.mechanical_engineering
=====================================


.. include:: /_generated/nav/mechanical_engineering.rst

Moments of inertia and the parallel-axis theorem, work, kinetic energy,
power and flywheels; gear trains and the Euler-Eytelwein belt-friction law;
Coulomb's square-thread power screw, Petroff's journal-bearing torque, the
Sommerfeld number and long-bearing eccentricity, and the Lundberg-Palmgren
:math:`L_{10}` life; the slider-crank, Grashof's condition, four-bar loop
closure and Freudenstein synthesis, and Denavit-Hartenberg forward
kinematics; the damped oscillator (``scipy.integrate.solve_ivp``),
transmissibility, Frahm and Den Hartog vibration absorbers and Holzer's
torsional method (``scipy.optimize.brentq``); and the Jeffcott rotor.
