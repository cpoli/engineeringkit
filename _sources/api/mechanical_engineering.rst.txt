engineeringkit.mechanical_engineering
=====================================


.. include:: /_generated/nav/mechanical_engineering.rst

Moments of inertia and the parallel-axis theorem, work, kinetic energy,
power and flywheels, and Lagrange's equations by numerical differentiation;
gear trains, the Lewis bending stress with Barth's factor and the
Euler-Eytelwein belt-friction law; Coulomb's square-thread power screw,
Petroff's journal-bearing torque, the Sommerfeld number and long-bearing
eccentricity, the Lundberg-Palmgren :math:`L_{10}` life, shafts in combined
bending and torsion (static and Goodman), helical springs with the Wahl
factor and preloaded bolted joints; the slider-crank, Grashof's condition,
four-bar loop closure, velocity and acceleration analysis and Freudenstein
synthesis, cam displacement laws and Denavit-Hartenberg forward kinematics;
the damped oscillator (``scipy.integrate.solve_ivp``), the forced
magnification factor and phase, transmissibility, normal modes
(``scipy.linalg.eigh``) and modal superposition, Rayleigh's and Dunkerley's
frequency bounds, Frahm and Den Hartog vibration absorbers and Holzer's
torsional method (``scipy.optimize.brentq``); the Jeffcott rotor and
one- and two-plane balancing.
