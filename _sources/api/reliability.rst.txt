engineeringkit.reliability
==========================


.. include:: /_generated/nav/reliability.rst

Halley's life table and the bathtub hazard; Poisson failure counts;
exponential, Weibull, lognormal and gamma lifetimes, Epstein-Sobel MTBF
confidence bounds (``scipy.stats.chi2``) and the Kaplan-Meier estimator for
censored data; median ranks, Weibull rank regression and censored maximum
likelihood; Arrhenius, Eyring and inverse-power-law acceleration factors;
gamma-Poisson Bayesian updating of a failure rate; series, parallel,
k-out-of-n and cold-standby systems, minimal path and cut sets of general
block diagrams and Esary-Proschan bounds, with Birnbaum importance;
Freudenthal's stress-strength interference, the Hasofer-Lind index and
Monte Carlo with importance sampling; FMEA risk priority numbers and
criticality, fault trees with repeated events, event trees and IEC 61508
safety integrity levels; Markov availability (``scipy.linalg.expm``),
Barlow-Proschan age replacement and Duane reliability growth.
