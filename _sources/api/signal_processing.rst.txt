engineeringkit.signal_processing
================================


.. include:: /_generated/nav/signal_processing.rst

Sampling, aliasing and sinc reconstruction; uniform quantization and SNR;
the DFT, a hand-written radix-2 FFT and Fourier partial sums; convolution
and correlation; the periodogram, windowed spectra, Welch's averaged
periodogram, the short-time Fourier transform and Goertzel's algorithm;
Butterworth filters as polynomials or second-order sections
(``scipy.signal``), window-method FIR filters with Kaiser's formulas; the FIR
Wiener filter (``scipy.linalg.solve_toeplitz``) and the LMS adaptive filter;
and the Haar wavelet transform.
