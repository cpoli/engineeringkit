engineeringkit.structural_engineering
=====================================


.. include:: /_generated/nav/structural_engineering.rst

Hooke's law, Poisson's ratio, Coulomb's torsion and Bredt's thin-walled
tubes for axial and twisted members; rectangular section properties,
elastic and plastic, and I, T, channel, angle, box and transformed sections
built from rectangles; Navier's bending stress, unsymmetrical bending,
Jourawski's shear stress and the shear centre; the simply supported beam,
Macaulay's beam on any supports and superposition, Clapeyron's three-moment
equation and influence lines; Euler's buckling load, Engesser's
tangent-modulus load, the secant formula and Johnson's parabola
(``scipy.optimize.brentq``); plane stress transformation, Mohr's circle,
three-dimensional principal stresses and strain rosettes; thin- and
thick-walled pressure vessels; Castigliano's theorem and the unit-load
method (``scipy.integrate.simpson``) and Maxwell-Betti reciprocity;
Maxwell's count, the method of joints and the direct stiffness truss with
conditioning diagnostics; plastic collapse loads; slope-deflection, Hardy
Cross's moment distribution and the plane-frame stiffness method; parabolic
and catenary cables and the three-hinged arch.
