engineeringkit.structural_engineering
=====================================


.. include:: /_generated/nav/structural_engineering.rst

Hooke's law, Poisson's ratio and Coulomb's torsion for axial and twisted
members; rectangular section properties, elastic and plastic; Navier's
bending stress and the simply supported beam, Clapeyron's three-moment
equation and influence lines; Euler's buckling load and Engesser's
tangent-modulus load (``scipy.optimize.brentq``); plane stress
transformation and Mohr's circle; Castigliano's theorem
(``scipy.integrate.simpson``) and Maxwell-Betti reciprocity; Maxwell's
count, the method of joints and the direct stiffness truss with
conditioning diagnostics; plastic collapse loads; Hardy Cross's moment
distribution and the plane-frame stiffness method.
