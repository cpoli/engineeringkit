engineeringkit.aerodynamics#

Domain hub · History · Examples · API reference

Dynamic pressure and force coefficients; Kutta-Joukowski circulation lift; thin-airfoil theory (with Glauert’s zero-lift and moment integrals by scipy.integrate.quad), the aerodynamic centre, NACA four-digit sections and the Hess-Smith panel method; Prandtl’s lifting line, Glauert’s Fourier solution for any planform, induced drag and the Oswald drag polar; static longitudinal stability; climb, turn, take-off and the jet and propeller Breguet range equations; Reynolds similarity and Blasius/Prandtl flat-plate friction; sound speed, the Mach cone, isentropic stagnation states, Prandtl-Glauert, normal and oblique shocks, Prandtl-Meyer expansions, the area-Mach relation, and Fanno and Rayleigh duct flow; and the International Standard Atmosphere. engineeringkit.aerodynamics: from dynamic pressure to finite wings and compressible flow.

Force coefficients and dynamic pressure; Kutta-Joukowski circulation lift; thin-airfoil theory (lift, pitching moment, aerodynamic centre), NACA four-digit sections and the Hess-Smith panel method; Prandtl’s lifting line, with Glauert’s Fourier solution for any planform, induced drag and the parabolic drag polar; static longitudinal stability; climb, turn, take-off and jet and propeller Breguet range; Reynolds-number similarity and Blasius/Prandtl flat-plate boundary layers; perfect-gas sound speed, isentropic stagnation states, the Mach cone, Prandtl-Glauert, Rankine-Hugoniot normal shocks, oblique shocks, Prandtl-Meyer expansions, the de Laval area-Mach relation, and Fanno and Rayleigh duct flow; and the International Standard Atmosphere. Models are hand-written closed forms or small linear solves (the formula is the pedagogical subject); SciPy is used only for quadrature and the inverse Prandtl-Meyer root.

engineeringkit.aerodynamics.fanno_flow(M, gamma=1.4)[source]#

Fanno-line ratios of adiabatic flow with wall friction in a constant-area duct.

\[\frac{T}{T^*} = \frac{\gamma+1}{2 + (\gamma-1)M^2},\qquad \frac{p}{p^*} = \frac1M\sqrt{\frac{T}{T^*}},\qquad \frac{f L^*}{D} = \frac{1 - M^2}{\gamma M^2} + \frac{\gamma+1}{2\gamma}\ln\frac{(\gamma+1)M^2}{2 + (\gamma-1)M^2}\]
Parameters:

  • M (float or array_like) – Mach number [-], positive.

  • gamma (float, optional) – Ratio of specific heats [-], greater than one.

Ratios to the sonic state, including the friction parameter \(f L^*/D\) with the Darcy friction factor \(f\) (four times the Fanning factor).

Friction drives subsonic flow up and supersonic flow down towards \(M = 1\), while \(T_0\) stays constant and \(p_0\) falls. Inputs are raw SI numbers with no dimensional metadata; see Aerodynamics: incompressible limits and compressible conservation.

References
