engineeringkit.communications: modulation, noise, capacity, detection, coding, fading and link budgets.

Hertz wavelengths and Friis free-space propagation with decibel link budgets; antenna gain, effective aperture and the half-wave dipole; Johnson-Nyquist thermal noise, Friis’s cascaded noise factor and the output SNR of AM and FM; Nyquist signalling, Hartley’s line rate, Shannon entropy and capacity, and Huffman coding; AM, suppressed-carrier and FM modulation with Carson’s bandwidth rule; raised-cosine pulse shaping; the matched filter, BPSK/QPSK, M-PSK and M-QAM and their AWGN error rates; PCM, mu-law companding and delta modulation; Hamming(7,4), CRC and convolutional codes with Viterbi decoding; Rayleigh fading and diversity combining; and DFT-based OFDM. Models are hand-written closed forms; NumPy’s FFT and SciPy’s erfc, sine/cosine integrals, quadrature and trapezoidal integration are used where they are the standard tool.

Returns:

One bit per sample; delta_demodulate() rebuilds the staircase.
