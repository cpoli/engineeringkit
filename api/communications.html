engineeringkit.communications#

Domain hub · History · Examples · API reference

Decibel conversions; wavelength, Friis free-space propagation and decibel link budgets; Johnson-Nyquist thermal noise and the cascaded noise factor; Nyquist signalling, Hartley’s law, binary entropy and Shannon capacity; AM, DSB-SC and FM modulation with Carson’s bandwidth rule; BPSK/QPSK and their AWGN bit-error rates (scipy.special.erfc); the Hamming(7,4) code; and OFDM via numpy.fft.

engineeringkit.communications: modulation, noise, capacity, detection, coding and link budgets.

Hertz wavelengths and Friis free-space propagation with decibel link budgets; Johnson-Nyquist thermal noise and Friis’s cascaded noise factor; Nyquist signalling, Hartley’s line rate, Shannon entropy and capacity; AM, suppressed-carrier and FM modulation with Carson’s bandwidth rule; BPSK/QPSK and their AWGN error rates; Hamming(7,4) error correction; and DFT-based OFDM. Models are hand-written closed forms; NumPy’s FFT and SciPy’s erfc and trapezoidal integration are used where they are the standard tool.

engineeringkit.communications.hamming74_decode(codewords)[source]#

Decode Hamming(7,4) codewords, correcting up to one flipped bit per word.

Parameters:

codewords (array_like) – Received binary words; length must be a multiple of seven.

Returns:

Decoded data bits, the syndrome of each word (the 1-based position of the corrected bit, or 0), and the corrected codewords.

Return type:

HammingDecodeResult

Notes

Two or more errors in one word are silently mis-corrected; the code’s minimum distance of three guarantees only single-error correction. See Communications: linear ratios, modulation phase and channel scope.

Examples

>>> received = hamming74_encode([1, 0, 1, 1])
>>> received[4] ^= 1
>>> result = hamming74_decode(received)
>>> result.data.tolist(), result.syndromes.tolist()
([1, 0, 1, 1], [5])
