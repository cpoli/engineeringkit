Examples#

Domain hub · History · Examples · API reference

This gallery walks through every public feature of engineeringkit.heat_transfer, one breakthrough per script: Newton’s cooling and the convection correlations, transient conduction from Biot to Heisler, Fourier’s law, fins and finite differences, blackbody radiation and view factors, phase change, and heat exchangers.

Units, assumptions and numerical validation gives the physical validity limits. Each script is self-contained and can be run directly with python examples/heat_transfer/<section>/<script>.py.

Sections#

  • convection – Newton’s law of cooling, the Dittus-Boelter and Churchill-Chu correlations.

  • transient – Biot’s number, Kelvin’s age of the Earth and Heisler’s charts.

  • conduction – Fourier’s law, Harper and Brown’s fins and the Crank-Nicolson scheme.

  • radiation – Kirchhoff’s law, Stefan-Boltzmann, Wien’s displacement and Nusselt’s view factors.

  • phase_change – Stefan’s ice problem, Nusselt’s film condensation and the boiling curve.

  • exchangers – Kays and London’s effectiveness-NTU method.
