Examples#

Domain hub · History · Examples · API reference

This gallery walks through every public feature of engineeringkit.reliability, one breakthrough per script: lifetime distributions and censored data, series, parallel and k-out-of-n systems, stress-strength and first-order structural reliability, fault and event trees, availability and replacement, and reliability growth.

Units, assumptions and numerical validation gives the validity limits. Each script is self-contained and can be run directly with python examples/reliability/<section>/<script>.py.

Sections#

  • lifetimes – Halley’s life table, Poisson’s rare failures, Weibull’s distribution, Epstein and Sobel’s life testing and the Kaplan-Meier estimator.

  • systems – Von Neumann’s redundancy, the AGREE report’s series systems, k-out-of-n systems and Birnbaum’s importance.

  • structural – Freudenthal’s stress-strength interference and Hasofer and Lind’s reliability index.

  • risk – Watson’s fault tree, WASH-1400’s event trees and IEC 61508’s safety integrity levels.

  • maintenance – Barlow and Proschan’s age replacement and Markov availability.

  • growth – Duane’s reliability growth.
