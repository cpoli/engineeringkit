Examples#

Domain hub · History · Examples · API reference

This gallery walks through every public feature of engineeringkit.structural_engineering, one breakthrough per script: axial and torsional members, beams, column buckling, stress transformation, energy methods, trusses, plastic collapse and frames.

Units, assumptions and numerical validation gives the physical validity limits. Each script is self-contained and can be run directly with python examples/structural_engineering/<section>/<script>.py.

Sections#

  • beams – Galileo’s cantilever, Navier’s bending theory, Clapeyron’s three-moment equation and Winkler’s influence lines.

  • members – Hooke’s law, Coulomb’s torsion and Poisson’s ratio.

  • stability – Euler’s column and Engesser’s tangent-modulus load.

  • stress – Mohr’s circle.

  • energy – Castigliano’s theorem.

  • trusses – Whipple’s method of joints, Maxwell’s count and reciprocal theorem, and the direct stiffness method.

  • plasticity – Kazinczy’s plastic hinges.

  • frames – Hardy Cross’s moment distribution and plane frames by direct stiffness.
