Examples#

Domain hub · History · Examples · API reference

This gallery walks through every public feature of engineeringkit.thermodynamics, one breakthrough per script: the first and second laws, ideal and real gases and their mixtures, vapour pressure and steam properties, heat-engine cycles and power plants, refrigerators and heat pumps, exergy, moist air and combustion.

Units, assumptions and numerical validation gives the physical validity limits. Each script is self-contained and can be run directly with python examples/thermodynamics/<section>/<script>.py.

Sections#

  • laws – Joule’s mechanical equivalent of heat and steady-flow devices, Clausius’s entropy and Maxwell’s relations.

  • properties – Dalton’s partial pressures, Clapeyron’s ideal gas, the Clausius-Clapeyron relation, the Joule-Thomson effect, van der Waals’s equation, Antoine’s equation, Pitzer’s acentric factor, Peng and Robinson’s equation and IAPWS-IF97 steam properties up to region 5.

  • cycles – Carnot’s bound, Stirling’s regenerator, the Rankine, Brayton, Otto, Diesel and dual cycles with reheat, feedwater heating, regeneration and intercooling, and the Curzon-Ahlborn efficiency at maximum power.

  • refrigeration – Perkins’s vapour-compression refrigerator, on water and on R-134a, and Kelvin’s heat pump.

  • exergy – Gouy and Stodola’s lost work and Rant’s exergy.

  • psychrometrics – Carrier’s psychrometric chart and air-conditioning processes.

  • combustion – Hess’s law, heating values and the adiabatic flame temperature.
