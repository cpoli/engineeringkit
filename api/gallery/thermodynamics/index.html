Examples#

Domain hub · History · Examples · API reference

This gallery walks through every public feature of engineeringkit.thermodynamics, one breakthrough per script: the first and second laws, ideal and real gases, vapour pressure and steam properties, heat-engine cycles, refrigerators and heat pumps, exergy and moist air.

Units, assumptions and numerical validation gives the physical validity limits. Each script is self-contained and can be run directly with python examples/thermodynamics/<section>/<script>.py.

Sections#

  • laws – Joule’s mechanical equivalent of heat and Clausius’s entropy.

  • properties – Clapeyron’s ideal gas, the Clausius-Clapeyron relation, the Joule-Thomson effect, van der Waals’s equation, Antoine’s equation and IAPWS-IF97 steam properties.

  • cycles – Carnot’s bound, the Rankine, Brayton, Otto and Diesel cycles, and the Curzon-Ahlborn efficiency at maximum power.

  • refrigeration – Perkins’s vapour-compression refrigerator and Kelvin’s heat pump.

  • exergy – Gouy and Stodola’s lost work and Rant’s exergy.

  • psychrometrics – Carrier’s psychrometric chart.
