Integrate Paris’s law from an initial to a final crack length.

\[N = \int_{a_0}^{a_f} \frac{da}{C\,(Y(a)\,\Delta\sigma\sqrt{\pi a})^m}\]
Parameters:

  • C (float) – Paris coefficient [m/cycle per (Pa sqrt(m))^m], positive.

  • m (float) – Paris exponent [-], positive.

  • af (float) – Final crack length [m], greater than a0; often critical_crack_length() at the peak stress.

  • Y (float | Callable) – Geometry factor [-], a positive constant or a function Y(a) of crack length [m] (for example a finite-width correction).

  • points (int) – Number of geometrically spaced crack lengths, at least 2.

Returns:

Crack length against cycles.

Return type:

CrackGrowthResult

Notes

All parameters are scalar-only: the result is one integrated crack-growth history.

The trapezoid rule on a geometric grid; with a constant Y and \(m \ne 2\) the closed form \(N = 2(a_0^{1-m/2} - a_f^{1-m/2}) / ((m - 2) C (Y\Delta\sigma\sqrt\pi)^m)\) checks it.
