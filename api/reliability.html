engineeringkit.reliability.adjusted_ranks(times, failed=None)[source]#

Johnson’s adjusted order numbers of the failures in a censored sample.

Units are sorted by time. At the unit in position \(j\) with reverse rank \(r = n - j + 1\), a failure gets the order number \(O_j = (r\,O_{prev} + n + 1)/(r + 1)\), where \(O_{prev}\) is that of the previous failure (zero at first). A censored unit takes no order number but makes every later increment larger, since it might have failed anywhere after its removal. Without censoring the order numbers are 1, 2, ..., n.

Parameters:

  • times (array_like) – Failure or censoring time of each unit [s], positive.

  • failed (array_like of {0, 1} or bool, optional) – 1 where the unit failed, 0 where it was censored. Default: every unit failed. At a tie, failures are ranked first.

Returns:

(failure_times, order): the failure times in increasing order [s] and their adjusted order numbers [-], for median_ranks().

Return type:

tuple of ndarray

Notes

Inputs are raw SI numbers with no dimensional metadata; see Reliability: distributions, independence and rate units.

References

L. G. Johnson, The Statistical Treatment of Fatigue Experiments (Elsevier, Amsterdam, 1964), ch. 3.

Examples

>>> failure_times, order = adjusted_ranks([10, 20, 30, 40], [1, 0, 1, 1])
>>> np.round(order, 3)
array([1.   , 2.333, 3.667])

Notes

engineeringkit.reliability.weibull_mle(times, failed=None, ranks='exact')[source]#

Maximum-likelihood Weibull scale and shape from complete or censored life data.

With \(r\) failures among \(n\) units and every unit’s time \(t_i\) (failure or censoring), the shape solves Cohen’s equation

\[\frac{\sum_{i=1}^n t_i^\beta \ln t_i}{\sum_{i=1}^n t_i^\beta} - \frac{1}{\beta} - \frac{1}{r}\sum_{\text{failures}} \ln t_i = 0,\]

whose left side increases with \(\beta\), and then \(\eta = (\sum_i t_i^\beta / r)^{1/\beta}\).

Parameters:

  • times (array_like) – Failure or censoring time of each unit [s], positive.

  • failed (array_like of {0, 1} or bool, optional) – 1 where the unit failed, 0 where it was censored. Default: every unit failed.

  • ranks ({"exact", "benard"}, optional) – Median-rank method for the plotted points of the result; the fit does not use them.

Returns:

Scale, shape and the points for plot().

Return type:

WeibullFitResult

Raises:

ValueError – With no failures, or when every failure is at the longest time on test (the likelihood then grows without bound in \(\beta\)).

Notes

Times are scaled by the longest one before solving, which leaves the equation unchanged and keeps \(t^\beta\) from overflowing. Inputs are raw SI numbers with no dimensional metadata; see Reliability: distributions, independence and rate units.

References

A. C. Cohen, “Maximum likelihood estimation in the Weibull distribution based on complete and on censored samples,” Technometrics 7 (1965) 579-588.
