class engineeringkit.structural_engineering.Beam(length, young_modulus, second_moment, supports)[source]#

Bases: object

Prismatic Euler-Bernoulli beam on any supports, solved by Macaulay’s method.

The bending moment is written once for the whole beam with singularity functions \(\langle x - a\rangle^n\), which are zero to the left of \(a\):

\[M(x) = \sum R_k\langle x - a_k\rangle - \sum P\langle x - a\rangle + \sum C\langle x - a\rangle^0 - \tfrac{w}{2}\langle x - a\rangle^2 + \cdots\]

and integrated twice, \(EIy = \iint M\,dx\,dx + C_1x + C_2\). The unknown reactions and the two constants follow from vertical and moment equilibrium and the support conditions (\(y = 0\) at every support, \(y' = 0\) at a fixed one), so statically indeterminate beams are solved the same way as determinate ones.

Parameters:

  • length (float) – Length [m], positive; the beam runs from \(x = 0\) to length.

  • young_modulus (float) – Young’s modulus [Pa], positive.

  • second_moment (float) – Second moment of area [m^4], positive.

  • supports (tuple) – (position [m], kind) for each support, with kind one of "pin", "roller" (both restrain deflection only) or "fixed" (restrains deflection and slope).

References

W. H. Macaulay, “Note on the deflection of beams,” Messenger of Mathematics 48 (1919) 129-130; A. Clebsch, Theorie der Elasticität fester Körper (Leipzig: Teubner, 1862).

engineeringkit.structural_engineering.castigliano_deflection(dU_dP=None, *, x=None, moment=None, dmoment_dP=None, E=None, I=None)[source]#

Deflection by Castigliano’s second theorem.

Called with a single number, it returns that strain-energy derivative \(\partial U/\partial P\) unchanged (the original interface). Called with the keyword arguments, it integrates

\[\delta = \int \frac{M(x)}{E I(x)}\,\frac{\partial M}{\partial P}(x)\,dx\]

along a beam with Simpson’s rule, where \(M\) is the bending moment under the actual loads and \(\partial M/\partial P\) the moment produced by a unit load at the point and in the direction of the wanted deflection (a dummy load if no real load acts there).

Parameters:

  • dU_dP (float, optional) – Strain-energy derivative [m], returned unchanged.

  • x (array_like, optional) – Stations along the beam [m], strictly increasing. An odd number of equally spaced stations, with a station at every load point, integrates piecewise-cubic integrands exactly.

  • moment (array_like, optional) – Bending moment at each station [N m].

  • dmoment_dP (array_like, optional) – Derivative of the moment with respect to the load [m] (N m/N).

  • E (float or array_like, optional) – Young’s modulus [Pa], positive.

  • I (float or array_like, optional) – Second moment of area [m^4], positive; may vary along the beam.

Returns:

Deflection [m] in the direction of the load P.

Return type:

float

Notes

Assumptions, governing equations and validity limits are discussed in Structural engineering: loads, signs and linear elasticity. SI inputs are required; raw numbers carry no dimensional metadata. Only bending energy is included; axial and shear energy are negligible for slender beams.

References

A. Castigliano, Théorie de l’équilibre des systèmes élastiques et ses applications (Turin: Negro, 1879).
