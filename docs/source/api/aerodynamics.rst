engineeringkit.aerodynamics
===========================


.. include:: /_generated/nav/aerodynamics.rst

Dynamic pressure and force coefficients; Kutta-Joukowski circulation lift;
thin-airfoil theory (with Glauert's zero-lift and moment integrals by
``scipy.integrate.quad``), the aerodynamic centre, NACA four-digit sections
and the Hess-Smith panel method; Prandtl's lifting line, Glauert's Fourier
solution for any planform, induced drag and the Oswald drag polar; static
longitudinal stability; climb, turn, take-off and the jet and propeller
Breguet range equations; Reynolds similarity and Blasius/Prandtl flat-plate
friction; sound speed, the Mach cone, isentropic stagnation states,
Prandtl-Glauert, normal and oblique shocks, Prandtl-Meyer expansions, the
area-Mach relation, and Fanno and Rayleigh duct flow; and the International
Standard Atmosphere. Models are hand-written closed forms or small linear
solves, because the formula is the subject.
