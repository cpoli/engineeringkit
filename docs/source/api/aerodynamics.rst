engineeringkit.aerodynamics
===========================


.. include:: /_generated/nav/aerodynamics.rst

Dynamic pressure and force coefficients; Kutta-Joukowski circulation lift;
thin-airfoil theory (with Glauert's zero-lift integral by
``scipy.integrate.quad``) and NACA four-digit sections; Prandtl's lifting
line, induced drag, the Oswald drag polar and the Breguet range equation;
Reynolds similarity and Blasius/Prandtl flat-plate friction; sound speed,
the Mach cone, isentropic stagnation states, Prandtl-Glauert, normal shocks
and the area-Mach relation; and the International Standard Atmosphere. All
models are hand-written closed forms, because the formula is the subject.
