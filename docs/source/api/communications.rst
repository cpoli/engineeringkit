engineeringkit.communications
=============================


.. include:: /_generated/nav/communications.rst

Decibel conversions; wavelength, Friis free-space propagation and decibel
link budgets; antenna gain, effective aperture, directivity and the
half-wave dipole (``scipy.special.sici``); Johnson-Nyquist thermal noise,
the cascaded noise factor and the output SNR of AM and FM; Nyquist
signalling and raised-cosine pulse shaping, Hartley's law, entropy, Huffman
coding and Shannon capacity; AM, DSB-SC and FM modulation with Carson's
bandwidth rule; the matched filter, BPSK/QPSK, Gray-labelled M-PSK and
square M-QAM and their AWGN error rates (``scipy.special.erfc``, Craig's
integral by ``scipy.integrate.quad``); PCM, mu-law companding and delta
modulation; the Hamming(7,4) code, cyclic redundancy checks, and
convolutional codes with Viterbi decoding; Rayleigh fading and diversity
combining; and OFDM via ``numpy.fft``.
