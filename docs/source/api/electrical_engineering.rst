engineeringkit.electrical_engineering
=====================================

.. include:: /_generated/nav/electrical_engineering.rst

Ohm's law, series and parallel equivalents, the Wheatstone bridge,
modified nodal analysis and mesh analysis; Thévenin and Norton equivalents
and maximum power transfer; RC and RL transients and the second-order RLC
step; phasors, complex impedance, series resonance and the ideal
transformer; ideal op-amp circuits, the Shockley diode and its load line;
DC machine and induction motor torque-speed curves; lossless and lossy
transmission lines, the distortionless condition, reflection coefficients
and input impedance; real, reactive and three-phase power, the per-unit
system, symmetrical components and fault currents, Gauss-Seidel and
Newton-Raphson load flow, the swing equation and the equal-area criterion
(linear systems by ``numpy.linalg``, the RLC step response by
``scipy.signal``, roots by ``scipy.optimize`` and swing curves by
``engineeringkit.integrators``).
