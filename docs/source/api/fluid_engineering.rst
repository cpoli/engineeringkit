engineeringkit.fluid_engineering
================================


.. include:: /_generated/nav/fluid_engineering.rst

Hydrostatic pressure, buoyancy and the force and centre of pressure on
submerged planes; Bernoulli's equation and continuity, with Pitot and
Venturi meters; Reynolds number, Hagen-Poiseuille flow and its velocity
profile, Darcy-Weisbach friction and minor losses, and the smooth,
Nikuradse fully rough, Colebrook (``scipy.optimize.brentq``) and
Swamee-Jain friction factors; Froude, Weber, Euler and Mach numbers and
Buckingham's Pi theorem by exact rational row reduction; Manning's
open-channel formula; Joukowsky's water hammer with Korteweg's wave speed;
pump power, specific speed, NPSH and Thoma's cavitation parameter; and
Hardy Cross's balancing of looped pipe networks.
