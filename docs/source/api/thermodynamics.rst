engineeringkit.thermodynamics
=============================


.. include:: /_generated/nav/thermodynamics.rst

Ideal and van der Waals gases, the van der Waals critical point and the
Joule-Thomson coefficient; Joule's first law and Clausius's entropy; the
Carnot bound, the Otto, Diesel, Brayton and Rankine cycles and the
Curzon-Ahlborn efficiency at maximum power; refrigerator and heat-pump
COPs; Gouy-Stodola lost work and the exergy of heat; vapour pressure by
Clausius-Clapeyron and Antoine; water and steam properties from IAPWS-IF97
regions 1, 2 and 4 (inverted for entropy and enthalpy with
``scipy.optimize.brentq``); and Carrier's psychrometrics.
