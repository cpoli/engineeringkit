engineeringkit.thermodynamics
=============================


.. include:: /_generated/nav/thermodynamics.rst

Ideal, van der Waals and Peng-Robinson gases, the compressibility factor,
Pitzer's acentric factor and the Joule-Thomson coefficient; Dalton's
ideal-gas mixtures and Maxwell's relations; Joule's first law for closed
systems and steady-flow devices, with isentropic efficiencies, and
Clausius's entropy; the Carnot bound, the Otto, Diesel, dual, Brayton,
Stirling and Rankine cycles, Rankine plants with reheat and feedwater
heating, gas turbines with regeneration, intercooling and reheat, and the
Curzon-Ahlborn efficiency at maximum power; refrigerator and heat-pump COPs
and the vapour-compression cycle on R-134a; Gouy-Stodola lost work and the
exergy of heat; vapour pressure by Clausius-Clapeyron and Antoine; water and
steam properties from all five regions of IAPWS-IF97 (inverted for entropy
and enthalpy with ``scipy.optimize.brentq``); Carrier's psychrometrics and
air-conditioning processes; and combustion stoichiometry, heating values and
the adiabatic flame temperature.
