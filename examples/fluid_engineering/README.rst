Examples
========

.. include:: /_generated/nav/fluid_engineering.rst

This gallery walks through every public feature of
``engineeringkit.fluid_engineering``, one breakthrough per script:
hydrostatics, flow meters, orifices and weirs, the momentum of jets, pipe
friction from Hagen and Poiseuille to Swamee and Jain, Couette flow, drag
on spheres, ship models and dimensional analysis, open channels, water
hammer, turbomachines, pump cavitation and pipe networks.

:doc:`Units, assumptions and numerical validation </guides/fluid_engineering>`
gives the physical validity limits. Each script is self-contained and can be
run directly with ``python examples/fluid_engineering/<section>/<script>.py``.

Sections
--------

- **hydrostatics** -- Archimedes's principle and Stevin's hydrostatic paradox.
- **measurement** -- Pitot's tube, Venturi's meter, Torricelli's orifice and Thomson's V-notch weir.
- **momentum** -- Pelton's water wheel.
- **pipe_flow** -- Hagen-Poiseuille, Darcy-Weisbach, Reynolds's transition, Nikuradse's roughness, Colebrook's equation, Moody's diagram and Swamee-Jain.
- **viscous** -- Couette's rotating-cylinder viscometer.
- **drag** -- Stokes's drag on a sphere.
- **similarity** -- Froude's ship models and Buckingham's Pi theorem.
- **open_channel** -- Manning's formula, Bélanger's hydraulic jump and Bakhmeteff's specific energy.
- **transients** -- Joukowsky's water hammer.
- **pumps** -- Thoma's cavitation parameter and Euler's turbine equation.
- **networks** -- Hardy Cross's method for pipe networks.
