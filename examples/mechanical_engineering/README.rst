Examples
========

.. include:: /_generated/nav/mechanical_engineering.rst

This gallery walks through every public feature of
``engineeringkit.mechanical_engineering``, one breakthrough per script:
rigid-body dynamics, gears and belts, screws and bearings, linkages and robot
arms, vibration and absorbers, and the whirling rotor.

:doc:`Units, assumptions and numerical validation </guides/mechanical_engineering>`
gives the physical validity limits. Each script is self-contained and can be
run directly with ``python examples/mechanical_engineering/<section>/<script>.py``.

Sections
--------

- **dynamics** -- Huygens's moment of inertia and Coriolis's work and kinetic energy.
- **transmission** -- Euler's involute gear and the Euler-Eytelwein belt-friction law.
- **machine_elements** -- Coulomb's power screw, Petroff's and Sommerfeld's journal bearings and the L10 life of rolling bearings.
- **mechanisms** -- Reuleaux's slider-crank, Grashof's condition, Freudenstein's synthesis and Denavit-Hartenberg kinematics.
- **vibration** -- Rayleigh's free vibration, Frahm's absorber and Holzer's torsional method.
- **rotordynamics** -- the Jeffcott rotor and its critical speed.
