Fluid engineering: flow regimes and signed quantities#

Density is kg/m^3, dynamic viscosity Pa s, velocity m/s, pressure Pa and all lengths/head m. Reynolds number represents speed: the function returns the magnitude rho*abs(v)*D/mu. Pressure changes, Poiseuille flow and water-hammer velocity changes may be signed. Bernoulli assumes steady incompressible flow along a streamline with no losses, pump or turbine work.

Internal flow#

Poiseuille uses a circular pipe, Newtonian fluid, fully developed laminar flow: Q=pi*r^4*delta_p/(8*mu*L). The Darcy factor is 64/Re below Re=2300; this is not the Fanning factor (one quarter as large). Colebrook uses the laminar branch below 2300 and a turbulent branch from 4000; transition is rejected explicitly. Relative roughness is epsilon/D, bounded here to [0,0.05]. Swamee-Jain is restricted to Re between 5000 and 1e8 for this implementation. These numeric contracts are model scope choices, not universal sharp physical transitions.

from engineeringkit.fluid_engineering import colebrook_friction_factor
f = colebrook_friction_factor(1e5, 1e-4)
# f is approximately 0.018514, dimensionless Darcy friction factor.

The tests integrate a parabolic velocity profile to recover Q, check that Poiseuille pressure drop equals Darcy loss in the laminar regime, and substitute computed f into the implicit Colebrook equation across multiple regimes.

The friction factors accept arrays and broadcast Re against the relative roughness; an array touching the 2300-4000 transition is rejected as a whole. smooth_pipe_friction_factor (Prandtl-Karman) needs Re >= 4000. nikuradse_friction_factor is the fully rough limit for sand-grain roughness in (0, 0.05]; it applies once Re*(k/D)*sqrt(f/8) exceeds about 70. Swamee-Jain is within 3% of Colebrook in its range, usually within 1%. pipe_resistance writes Darcy-Weisbach as h = r*Q*abs(Q) with f held fixed.

Hydrostatics#

hydrostatic_force_plane is the gauge-pressure resultant on a plane of any inclination: rho*g*(depth of centroid)*area. center_of_pressure_depth measures distances along the plane from the free-surface line (depths for a vertical surface) and needs the second moment about the horizontal centroidal axis.

Measurement and pumps#

Venturi assumes horizontal incompressible flow, positive upstream-minus- throat pressure difference, and 0<A2<A1; the optional discharge coefficient in (0, 1] defaults to the ideal meter. Pitot uses positive stagnation-minus-static pressure and is within 1% in air below about Mach 0.28. Cavitation number may be negative when p<p_vapor; nonzero speed is required. Pump helpers are forward pumping models with nonnegative volume flow/head and 0<efficiency<=1. Pump specific speed is the dimensionless angular-speed definition using rad/s; it differs from dimensional industry conventions based on rpm or feet. npsh_available takes the absolute static pressure at the inlet; thoma_sigma is NPSH over machine head.

Similarity, channels, transients and networks#

buckingham_pi takes each variable’s dimensions as a mapping of base dimension to integer exponent. The repeating variables must number the rank of the dimension matrix and be independent; each group then contains one other variable to a positive power. The exponents are exact integers from rational row reduction. Euler number here is dp/(rho*v^2), without the factor 1/2 some texts use.

manning_velocity uses the SI coefficient 1 (US customary units need 1.486) and assumes uniform, fully rough flow. pipe_wave_speed is Korteweg’s thin-wall formula; c1 = 1 for expansion joints, 1 - nu^2 for an anchored pipe, 0 for a rigid pipe. water_hammer_pressure holds for closures shorter than critical_closure_time = 2L/a and neglects friction.

hardy_cross needs initial flows that already satisfy continuity at every junction and a loop matrix with entries -1, 0, +1. It sweeps the loops in order (Gauss-Seidel), stops when the largest correction is below tol, and raises RuntimeError if max_iter sweeps are not enough. Convergence is linear, so loop residuals are of order the last correction times the loop resistance, not zero.

The rebuild’s tests integrate pressure over a submerged wall, integrate the Poiseuille profile, check the smooth and rough limits of Colebrook, the Chezy form of Manning, Korteweg’s rigid-pipe limit, the agreement of NPSH and cavitation number, Hardy Cross against a direct Newton solve, and that every Pi group lies in the null space of the dimension matrix.
